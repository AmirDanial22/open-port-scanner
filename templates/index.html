How to Find Your IP Address

To perform a scan, you need a target IP address. Depending on what network resource you want to test, check the instructions below:

  1. Press Win + R, type cmd, and press Enter to open Command Prompt.
  2. Type the following command and press Enter:
    ipconfig
  3. Look for IPv4 Address under your active network adapter (e.g. Wi-Fi or Ethernet). It usually starts with 192.168. or 10..
