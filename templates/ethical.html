Ethical Use Policy

Core principles for responsible network scanning.

1
Ownership & Permission

Only scan IP addresses and networks that you own or have explicit written permission to test.

2
Education Only

This tool is designed to teach about exposure and security posture — not to facilitate unauthorized access.

3
No Exploitation

This scanner only "knocks" on the door to check if it's open. It does not attempt to pick the lock or enter.

"With great power comes great responsibility. Curiosity is the foundation of learning, but respect for others' privacy and property is the foundation of community."
