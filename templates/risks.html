Risk Classification

A simplified educational scale to help you prioritize findings. No complex scoring — just clear, actionable categories.

🔴

High Risk Attention Recommended

Definition: Services that typically grant control over the system or direct access to raw data.

Examples: Remote Desktop, database direct access, unencrypted file transfer protocols.

Action: Ask yourself — "Does the entire internet need access to this?"
🟡

Medium Risk Review Needed

Definition: Useful but complex services that may leak system information or run software needing frequent updates.

Examples: Email servers, specialized web administration tools.

Action: Ensure software on these ports is up-to-date and patched.
🟢

Low Risk Informational

Definition: Standard services expected to be open on a web server, or services with very specific, limited use.

Examples: Public websites (HTTP/HTTPS).

Action: Normal behavior for a web server. No immediate concern.
